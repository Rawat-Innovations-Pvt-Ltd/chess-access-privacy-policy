Privacy Policy for Chess Access

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

Rawat Innovations Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of users of the Chess Access mobile application ("App").

This App is designed to work primarily offline and does not require account creation, sign-in, or internet access for core gameplay. We do not collect, store, or sell personal information from users for gameplay or app usage.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not intentionally collect the following information from App users:

  • Full name, phone number, or email address
  • Login credentials or account data
  • Contacts, address book, or social media data
  • Photos, media files, or microphone access
  • Location data or precise device GPS information
  • Game history, match data, or personal chess statistics unless the user chooses to save them locally on the device
  • Advertising identifiers or device tracking IDs for analytics or marketing

2. Offline Use and Local Storage

The App is designed to function offline. Game progress or settings may be stored locally on the device for convenience, but this information remains on the user's device and is not transmitted to us or any third-party service.

We do not use remote servers to store player profiles, game results, or chess data unless a future version of the App explicitly introduces a separate service with updated privacy disclosures.

3. No Analytics, Ads, or Third-Party Tracking

The App does not currently use advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or user tracking tools that collect personal information for behavioral profiling, marketing, or measurement.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may include third-party software libraries required for core functionality, platform support, or app performance. Such services are used only for technical operation of the App and do not require personal data from users.

If the App later introduces external services such as cloud storage, account systems, or analytics, we will update this Privacy Policy and provide notice before those features are enabled.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided personal information, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the App and user data from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. Because the App is designed to operate offline and store minimal data locally, the risk of personal data exposure is low.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in App functionality, legal requirements, or operational practices. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
